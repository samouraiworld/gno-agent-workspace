## Overview

Boards2 is gno.land's forum realm, and every board in it comes from `CreateBoard`, which only the realm owner or an admin may call. On a chain launched from this source the sole owner is the GovDAO multisig, so the forum starts empty and stays empty until several people sign one transaction. This change adds an `init()` that creates a single listed, open board named `OpenDiscussions` while the realm is being deployed, so the forum arrives with somewhere to post. To make that possible the board-building half of `CreateBoard` moves into an unexported helper, `addBoard`, which takes the creator address as an argument instead of reading the call frame and skips the user-registry lookup a deploy cannot perform. Two files carry the feature and the other 40 are filetests, all of which move because the new board consumes board ID 1 and appears on the front page.

**Verdict: REQUEST CHANGES** — the `addBoard` split drops no check `CreateBoard` performed and round 1's address objection is gone, but seeding an open board at genesis turns on a write path that carries no spam gate where its two siblings do (1 Warning, 1 Nit, 2 Suggestions).
