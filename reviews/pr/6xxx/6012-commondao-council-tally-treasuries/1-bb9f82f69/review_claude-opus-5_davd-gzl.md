## Summary

The PR deletes 2,121 lines of member-storage and grouping machinery and replaces the member model with a `*addrset.Set` Council, a single `TallyDefault` decided against a per-proposal electorate snapshot, and a per-DAO treasury address derived as a realm sub-identity (`chain.DerivePkgSubAddr(pkgPath, "dao/<id>")`). Nine proposal kinds are registered per DAO from a closed in-package catalog; the three fund-moving executors are the realm's only `SendCoins` call sites, and each receives a minted `cur.Sub(...)` whose authority the banker bounds to exactly one address. The treasury plumbing is the strongest part of the diff and it holds up under test: `NewBanker(BankerTypeRealmSend, sub)` rejects any `from` other than `sub.Address()` ([`banker.gno:170-172`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/blob/bb9f82f69/gnovm/stdlibs/chain/banker/banker.gno#L170-L172) · [↗](../../../../../.worktrees/gno-review-6012/gnovm/stdlibs/chain/banker/banker.gno#L170-L172)), and the derived address agrees with the minted sub because [`assertRunningPath`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/blob/bb9f82f69/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L33-L37) · [↗](../../../../../.worktrees/gno-review-6012/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L33-L37) fails the deploy otherwise. What does not hold up is the boundary around who may create a DAO, and the one place where the tree's fund-recovery invariant is written down as `parent.Address()` instead of "nearest live ancestor".

## Diagram

Where a treasury's coins can go, and the one edge that leaves the tree's recovery guarantee.

```
                    Root (alive)
                     |  ^
     dissolve(Root)  |  |  clawback(Mid)  -> Mid.Parent() = Root       OK
     -> destination  v  |  dissolve(Mid)  -> Mid.Parent() = Root       OK
                    Mid (DISSOLVED)
                     |  ^
                     |  |  clawback(Leaf) -> Leaf.Parent() = Mid    dead end
                     v  |  dissolve(Leaf) -> Leaf.Parent() = Mid    dead end  <-- Critical
                    Leaf (alive, holds coins)

Mid rejects Propose/Vote/Execute (deleted flag), so nothing spends from Mid.
A clawback on Mid still rescues to Root -- until Root dissolves too, and then
render.gno:86 prints "coins sent to this treasury address can no longer be
recovered" over funds the sweep itself put there.
```

## Fix

The dissolution sweep and the clawback both compute their destination as the target's immediate `parent.Address()`, which is correct only while that parent is alive. [`hasLiveProperAncestor`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/blob/bb9f82f69/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/proposal_treasury.gno#L16-L23) · [↗](../../../../../.worktrees/gno-review-6012/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/proposal_treasury.gno#L16-L23) already walks the chain that answers the question; returning the ancestor instead of a bool makes both destinations "the nearest live proper ancestor" and preserves the stated invariant that funds move toward their origin and stay reachable. For `New`, the two principals at [`public.gno:84`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/blob/bb9f82f69/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L84) · [↗](../../../../../.worktrees/gno-review-6012/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L84) and [`:91`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/blob/bb9f82f69/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L91) · [↗](../../../../../.worktrees/gno-review-6012/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L91) have to become one: either both read `cur.Previous().Address()`, as [`Invite`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/blob/bb9f82f69/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L49) · [↗](../../../../../.worktrees/gno-review-6012/examples/quarantined/gno.land/r/nt/commondao/v0/public.gno#L49) already does, or the entry additionally requires `cur.Previous().IsUserCall()` so origin and immediate caller cannot diverge.
